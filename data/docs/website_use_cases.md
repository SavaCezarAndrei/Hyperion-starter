The HYPER-AI consortium has strategically selected five big data processing applications across diverse, emerging sectors to foster industrial collaboration. These verticals demonstrate unique objectives, KPIs, and deployment nuances.

##
                  Mobility and Automotive

            Leveraging Idle Computing Power in Automated and Connected Vehicles

##
                  Industry 4.0

            AR-based and AI-augmented remote assembly and Robot-as-a-Service for improved process efficiency and workers’ safety

##
                  Healthcare

            Disease early diagnosis and control to prevent future pandemics

##
                  Green energy

            Energy efficient data processing simulation for monitoring of critical infrastructure

##
                  Farming and Agriculture

            Precision Agriculture improved by computing continuum from Cloud-to-edge-to-IoT
